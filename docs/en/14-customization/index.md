# Customization

The app's defaults are deliberately neutral, but you can tune the look and feel without changing how the app works.

- [Appearance](./appearance.md) — fonts, sizes, line height, paragraph spacing.
- [Color palettes](./color-palettes.md) — pick from eleven curated themes.
- [Theme toggle](./theme-toggle.md) — light, dark, or system-following.
- [Language](./language.md) — English or Vietnamese (more on the way).

All customization is **per-browser**. Settings live in `localStorage`, not in the vault — your notes are content; your styling is preference. Open the same vault in a different browser and you'll get the defaults until you tune them again.
