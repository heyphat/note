# Core concepts

A handful of decisions shape how Note feels. Most of the rest of the app falls out of them.

- [Local-first](./local-first.md) — no server, no account, your notes never leave your machine.
- [Vault layout](./vault-layout.md) — what the app puts on disk and where.
- [Markdown and frontmatter](./markdown-and-frontmatter.md) — the format every note uses, and what's in the YAML block at the top.
- [Cross-tab sync](./cross-tab-sync.md) — what happens when the same vault is open in two tabs.

If you only read one of these, read [Local-first](./local-first.md). It's the design choice every other section assumes.
